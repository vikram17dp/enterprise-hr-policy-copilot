## 1. New Employee Onboarding

HR should confirm the following before an employee begins:

1. Signed offer documentation.
2. Identity documentation.
3. Payroll documentation.
4. Manager assignment.
5. Start date.
6. Required policy acknowledgements.

On the first day, HR should provide or confirm:

- HR portal access.
- Benefits information.
- Mandatory training.
- Employee handbook.
- Manager and team information.
- Required onboarding documentation.

If an employee cannot access the HR portal on the first day, HR should verify the employee profile and coordinate with the appropriate support team.

---

## 2. Employee Leave Request Workflow

When an employee submits a leave request, HR or the HR system should verify:

1. Employee identity.
2. Active employee profile.
3. Requested leave type.
4. Requested dates.
5. Available leave balance where applicable.
6. Company holidays and weekly offs.
7. Required manager approval.
8. Any required supporting documentation.

The employee should normally submit planned leave through the HR portal.

Leave should not be considered approved until the required approval is recorded.

---

## 3. Leave Request Support

If an employee cannot submit leave through the HR portal:

### Step 1 — Verify Employee

Confirm that the employee has an active employee profile.

### Step 2 — Verify Leave Information

Check the applicable leave type and available balance if the HR system is accessible.

### Step 3 — Check Portal Availability

Determine whether the issue is:

- Employee-specific.
- System-wide.
- Temporary service interruption.

### Step 4 — Temporary Request Process

If the portal is unavailable, HR may record the request by email according to the applicable HR support procedure.

The manager should confirm approval through the approved communication channel.

### Step 5 — Update HR System

After the HR portal or HR system becomes available, HR should update the official HR system with the approved leave request.

The HR system remains the authoritative record.

---

## 4. Leave Request Escalation

If a leave request cannot be processed:

### Employee

The employee should contact their manager or HR support.

### Manager

The manager should confirm whether the requested absence can be approved based on business requirements.

### HR

HR should verify the applicable policy, employee record, leave balance, and system status.

If the issue cannot be resolved, HR should escalate it to the appropriate HR operations owner.

---

## 5. Emergency Leave

When an employee cannot submit leave in advance because of an unexpected event:

1. The employee should notify the manager as soon as reasonably possible.
2. HR should record the request through the available support process if the HR portal is unavailable.
3. The applicable leave type should be determined.
4. Required approval should be obtained.
5. The official HR system should be updated when available.

Emergency circumstances do not automatically mean that leave is approved.

---

## 6. Leave Balance Questions

When an employee asks:

- "How many leaves do I have?"
- "What is my remaining annual leave?"
- "How many sick leaves are left?"
- "Can I take 5 days based on my balance?"

HR should access an authorized employee-specific leave balance source.

The general HR handbook can explain leave entitlements, but it cannot provide an individual's current balance.

If no employee-specific leave data source is available, the HR Policy Copilot should not invent a balance.

---

## 7. Company Holiday Questions

When an employee asks whether a date is a company holiday:

1. Check the internal company holiday calendar.
2. Confirm the date and holiday name.
3. Confirm whether the holiday applies to the employee group where applicable.
4. Do not substitute a public internet holiday calendar for the company's internal holiday calendar.

Government or public holiday information may be consulted separately when the employee explicitly asks about statutory or external holidays.

---

## 8. Holiday vs Employee Leave

HR should distinguish between:

### Company Holiday

A company-designated non-working day.

No individual leave request is normally required.

### Weekly Off

A scheduled non-working day based on the employee's work schedule.

### Employee Leave

An absence requested by an individual employee and subject to the applicable approval process.

A company holiday or weekly off should not normally be deducted from an employee's leave balance.

The standard workflow is:

```text
Employee submits leave request
        ↓
HR system checks employee profile
        ↓
Applicable leave type identified
        ↓
Leave balance checked where applicable
        ↓
Manager reviews request
        ↓
Manager approves or rejects
        ↓
HR system records final status
