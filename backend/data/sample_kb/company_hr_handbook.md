# PeoplePrime Technologies — Internal HR Handbook

## 1. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive **20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year**.

Annual leave is intended for planned personal time, vacation, travel, and other personal requirements.

### Annual Leave Request

Employees should normally submit planned annual leave requests through the HR portal at least **5 working days before the planned leave date**.

Annual leave requires manager approval.

Employees should check their available leave balance before submitting a request.

Submitting a leave request does not mean the leave has been approved. Leave is considered approved only after the required approval is recorded in the HR system.

### Carry Forward

Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to **5 days** into the following calendar year.

Carried-forward leave must normally be used by **March 31** of the following year unless HR grants an exception.

### New Joiners

Employees joining during the calendar year may receive a prorated annual leave entitlement according to the applicable HR system calculation.

The HR system is the authoritative source for an employee's personal leave balance.

---

## 2. Casual Leave

Casual Leave (CL) is intended for short personal requirements that do not fall under sick leave or other specific leave categories.

Employees should normally request casual leave in advance whenever practical.

A maximum of **2 consecutive working days of Casual Leave** may normally be taken without additional manager approval beyond the standard leave workflow.

Longer periods require manager approval according to the applicable leave workflow.

Casual Leave is subject to the employee's available balance and applicable company rules.

---

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees may take up to **10 paid sick days per calendar year**.

Sick Leave is intended for illness or health-related inability to work.

Employees should notify their manager as early as reasonably possible on the first day of absence.

For absences longer than **2 consecutive working days**, HR may request appropriate medical documentation.

Employees should not use Sick Leave for planned vacation or personal travel when another applicable leave type should be used.

---

## 4. Earned / Privilege Leave

Earned Leave, also referred to as Privilege Leave (EL/PL), is intended primarily for planned personal time and vacation.

Employees should submit planned EL/PL requests through the designated HR or leave system in advance.

Approval depends on the employee's available balance, manager approval, and business requirements.

Employees should not assume that an EL/PL request is approved until the approval is recorded in the HR system.

---

## 5. Optional Holidays

Eligible employees may have access to Optional Holidays (OH) according to the applicable company calendar and employee group.

Optional Holidays must normally be selected or requested through the company's leave system.

Optional Holiday availability and entitlement may vary by employee group.

Employees should check the applicable company holiday calendar and their HR portal before requesting an Optional Holiday.

---

## 6. Company Holidays

Company holidays are company-designated non-working days.

Company holidays are different from employee leave.

An employee does not normally need to submit an individual leave request for a company holiday.

Company holidays are not normally deducted from an employee's leave balance.

The official company holiday calendar is the authoritative source for company-specific holiday dates.

If a company holiday occurs during an approved leave period, treatment follows the applicable company policy and employee work schedule.

---

## 7. Weekly Offs

Standard working hours are Monday through Friday.

Saturday and Sunday are normally non-working days unless an employee's approved work schedule specifies otherwise.

A weekly off is different from a company holiday and different from employee leave.

Weekly offs are not normally deducted from an employee's leave balance.

Employees working under alternate schedules should follow the weekly-off schedule recorded in the applicable HR system or approved work schedule.

---

## 8. Leave Request Rules

Employees should submit leave requests through the designated HR or leave system.

### Planned Leave

Planned leave should normally be submitted in advance.

Employees should check:

1. Available leave balance.
2. Applicable leave type.
3. Company holidays.
4. Weekly offs.
5. Required manager approval.

### Approval

Leave is subject to manager approval unless the applicable policy explicitly states otherwise.

Submitting a request does not automatically approve the leave.

Employees should wait for the approval status to be recorded in the HR system.

### Emergency Leave

When advance submission is not practical because of an unexpected situation, employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible and submit the leave request through the HR system when available.

---

## 9. Leave Balance

The HR system is the authoritative source for an employee's personal leave balance.

The HR Policy Copilot must not invent or estimate an employee's personal leave balance.

For questions such as:

- "How many annual leaves do I have?"
- "How much casual leave is remaining?"
- "How many sick leaves are left?"
- "Can I take 5 days of leave based on my balance?"

the Copilot may explain the general leave policy but must use an authorized employee-specific leave data source to provide an actual balance.

If employee-specific leave data is not connected, the Copilot should clearly state that it cannot verify the personal balance.

---

## 10. Leave Cancellation

Employees who no longer require approved leave should cancel or modify the leave request through the HR system when possible.

If the HR system does not allow the required change, the employee should contact HR and the manager.

Employees should not assume that an approved leave cancellation has been completed until the HR system reflects the updated status.

---

## 11. Leave During Company Holidays and Weekly Offs

Company holidays and weekly offs are normally treated as non-working days and are not deducted from employee leave balances.

For example, if an employee takes approved leave before and after a company holiday, the company holiday is normally treated separately from the employee's leave.

Employees should follow the official holiday calendar and their approved work schedule.

---

## 12. Working Hours and Attendance

Standard working hours are:

**9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday**

This includes a **1-hour meal break**.

Teams may use approved flexible schedules if business coverage is maintained.

Employees are expected to remain available during their agreed working hours.

Repeated unapproved absence or lateness should be discussed with the employee's manager and may be escalated to HR.

---

## 13. Attendance Corrections

If an employee notices an incorrect attendance record, the employee should report the issue through the applicable HR or attendance process.

Employees should provide the relevant date and explanation for the correction request.

Managers may be required to approve attendance corrections.

HR may review repeated attendance discrepancies.

---

## 14. Remote Work

Eligible employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week** with manager approval.

Employees working remotely must:

- Remain reachable during agreed working hours.
- Use company-approved collaboration tools.
- Protect confidential company information.
- Maintain an appropriate working environment.
- Follow attendance and availability requirements.

Remote work is not a substitute for dependent-care arrangements during working hours.

Remote work approval does not automatically change the employee's working hours.

---

## 15. Public Holidays and External Holidays

The company holiday calendar is the authoritative source for **PeoplePrime company holidays**.

Public holidays published by governments, states, or external websites should not automatically be treated as PeoplePrime company holidays.

If an employee asks whether a particular public holiday is a company holiday, the HR Copilot should first check the internal company holiday calendar.

External sources may be used for general statutory or government information when the user explicitly asks for current external requirements.

---

## 16. Payroll

Monthly salary is normally paid on the **final business day of each month**.

Employees should review their payslip in the HR portal after payroll processing.

Payroll discrepancies should be reported to Payroll Support within **5 business days** of receiving the payslip.

Examples of payroll issues include:

- Incorrect salary amount.
- Incorrect allowance.
- Incorrect deduction.
- Incorrect tax amount.
- Missing benefits deduction or contribution.

---

## 17. Benefits

Eligible full-time employees receive access to:

- Company medical plan.
- Life insurance coverage.
- Employee assistance program.

Enrollment changes outside the annual enrollment window generally require a qualifying life event and supporting documentation.

Employees should use the HR benefits system or contact HR for employee-specific enrollment information.

---

## 18. Employee Information Changes

Employees must update changes to important personal information through the HR portal.

Examples include:

- Legal name.
- Bank account details.
- Emergency contact.
- Home address.
- Other required employee records.

Supporting documentation may be required.

Bank-detail changes should be verified before the next payroll cutoff.

---

## 19. Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to maintain a respectful, professional, and harassment-free workplace.

The company does not permit:

- Discrimination.
- Retaliation.
- Bullying.
- Harassment.
- Other serious workplace misconduct.

Employees may report concerns to:

- Their manager.
- HR.
- The confidential ethics channel.

Retaliation against employees who raise concerns in good faith is prohibited.

---

## 20. HR Policy vs External Information

The HR Policy Copilot should clearly distinguish between internal company policy and external information.

### Internal HR Policy

Information about:

- PeoplePrime company holidays.
- PeoplePrime leave types.
- PeoplePrime leave approval.
- PeoplePrime working hours.
- PeoplePrime remote work.
- PeoplePrime payroll processes.
- PeoplePrime benefits.

should come from the internal HR knowledge base whenever available.

### External Information

Information about:

- Government notifications.
- Current statutory requirements.
- Changes in Indian labour regulations.
- Public or government holiday declarations.
- General employment law.

may require trusted external sources when current information is requested and the internal HR knowledge base does not contain it.

External information must not be presented as PeoplePrime company policy.

---

## 21. HR Copilot Safety Rules

The HR Copilot must not:

1. Invent an employee's leave balance.
2. Claim a leave request is approved without an authorized approval source.
3. Treat another employee's leave information as publicly available information.
4. Treat a public holiday as a PeoplePrime company holiday unless the internal company calendar confirms it.
5. Present external employment-law information as company policy.
6. Invent missing company policies.
7. Reveal confidential employee information.

When information is unavailable, the Copilot should clearly state what information it can verify and what information it cannot verify.
