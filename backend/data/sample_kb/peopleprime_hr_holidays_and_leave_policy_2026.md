## 2. Leave Types

### 2.1 Casual Leave (CL)

- Casual Leave may be used for short personal requirements.
- Employees should normally request CL in advance.
- A maximum of 2 consecutive working days of CL may be taken without additional manager approval.
- Longer periods require manager approval according to the leave workflow.

### 2.2 Sick Leave (SL)

- Sick Leave may be used when an employee is unable to work because of illness.
- Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.
- Supporting medical documentation may be requested for extended periods of absence.

### 2.3 Earned / Privilege Leave (EL/PL)

- Earned Leave is intended for planned personal or vacation time.
- Employees should submit planned leave requests in advance.
- Approval depends on business requirements and manager availability.

### 2.4 Optional Holiday (OH)

- Employees may be eligible for optional holidays according to the applicable company calendar.
- Optional holidays require selection/request through the company's leave system where applicable.
- Availability and entitlement may vary by employee group.

## 3. Leave Request Rules

1. Employees should submit leave requests through the designated HR/leave system.
2. Planned leave should be requested before the intended absence whenever practical.
3. Leave is subject to manager approval unless the applicable policy explicitly states otherwise.
4. Employees should check their available leave balance before submitting a request.
5. Company holidays and weekly offs are not normally deducted from an employee's leave balance.
6. If a company holiday falls during an approved leave period, treatment follows the applicable leave policy and employee work schedule.
7. Employees should not assume that a leave request is approved until approval is recorded in the official leave system.

## 4. Leave Balance

The HR Copilot must not invent an employee's personal leave balance.

For questions such as:

- "How many leaves do I have?"
- "How much casual leave is remaining?"
- "What is my current leave balance?"

the system should use an authorized employee leave/balance data source if one is connected.

If no employee-specific leave data source is available, the Copilot should state that it cannot verify the employee's current balance.

## 5. Company Holiday vs. Employee Leave

The HR Copilot should distinguish between:

**Company Holiday**
- A company-designated non-working day.
- Example: 02-Oct-2026 — Gandhi Jayanti.

**Employee Leave**
- Time off requested by an individual employee.
- Requires the applicable approval process.

The Copilot must never interpret "Are there any holidays next week?" as a request for another employee's private leave information.
